Wishly — Delete your data

Last updated 2026-10-01

Wishly has no account and keeps everything you add only on your phone, so you delete it yourself, on the phone, at any time. Nothing is held by us to request deletion of.

Delete everything in the app

Open Wishly → Settings → Your data → Delete everything, then tap “Yes, delete everything”.

This permanently removes every person, date, message and photo, your own details and settings, and any photo copies the app cached. It cannot be undone.

Delete only some of it

You can remove an address, a photo or a link between people from that person’s screen, and every saved phone number at once from Settings → Your data → Remove all phone numbers.

Uninstall

Uninstalling Wishly, or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Wishly → Storage, also deletes everything. Wishly is excluded from Android’s cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so nothing is restored if you install it again or copied to a new phone.
